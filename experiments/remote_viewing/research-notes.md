# Remote-viewing research review and preregistration notes
Status: 2026-10-08. Research review of accessible primary reports, book listings, practitioner methods and recent experiments. **Not** a claim to have read every copyrighted book in full.

## What historically merits testing
- Joseph McMoneagle: *Mind Trek* (1993); *The Ultimate Time Machine* (1998); *Remote Viewing Secrets* (2000); *The Stargate Chronicles* (2002). The methodological contributions here are disciplined session descriptions, separating protocol from technique, record preservation, blinding, and restraint about how impressions are scored. Published editions and excerpts: https://openlibrary.org/authors/OL40797A/Joseph_McMoneagle and https://books.google.com/books/about/Remote_Viewing_Secrets.html?id=3dNa0AEACAAJ
- Ingo Swann: SRI research and Coordinate Remote Viewing (CRV) structured descriptions, ideograms and stage descriptions; his books include *Everybody's Guide to Natural ESP*. CRV historical manual and attribution: https://centerlane-rv.org/manual; book catalog: https://books.google.com/books/about/Everybody_s_Guide_to_Natural_ESP.html?id=-mxuDwAAQBAJ. Specific claims about psychic perception remain unverified.
- Lyn Buchanan: *The Seventh Sense* (2003), procedural CRV training and the distinction between his proposed additions and the earlier Swann-derived manual: https://www.remoteviewed.com/remote-viewing-books/ and https://rviewer.com/controlled-remote-viewing-manual-background-and-overview/lyn-buchanans-draft-crv-manual-input/
- Paul H. Smith: Original CRV manual primary author, military operator and trainer; *Reading the Enemy's Mind* and *The Essential Guide to Remote Viewing*. Confirmed personal biography: https://rviewer.com/paul-h-smith/; Rice University archive: https://impossiblearchives.rice.edu/paul-h-smith. The name "Paul Booth" from the request did NOT match a verified Stargate investigator in the sources reviewed. It must not be silently equated to Smith without confirmation.
- Lyn Cassady is a fictional/composite character played by George Clooney in the 2009 satire *The Men Who Stare at Goats*. NOT evidence that passing through walls is the user's proposed experiment. Sources: https://www.rottentomatoes.com/m/men_who_stare_at_goats/cast-and-crew and Jon Ronson interview https://www.delawarepublic.org/2009-11-08/walking-through-walls-and-staring-at-goats

## Historical dispute: primary critiques on both sides
- Jessica Utts, *An Assessment of the Evidence for Psychic Functioning* (1995): argued statistical evidence in SRI/SAIC experiments warranted further research, with several statistically noteworthy experiments, and highlighted differences between dynamic/static targets. https://ics.uci.edu/~jutts/air.pdf
- Ray Hyman, *Evaluation of Program on Anomalous Mental Phenomena* (1995): praised improvements in SAIC methodology but disputed that effect sizes alone establish anomalous cognition, emphasizing the same investigators, judges and target pools, lack of independent replication, and need for rejudging by independent blind judges and multiple laboratories. https://ics.uci.edu/~jutts/hyman.html
- AIR/CIA evaluation, *An Evaluation of Remote Viewing: Research and Applications* (1995): separate experimental anomalous statistics from useful operational intelligence, which was judged unreliable. Official identifier: https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp96-00791r000200180006-4

## Modern findings that cannot be omitted
- 2024 *State, Trait, and Target Parameters Associated with Accuracy in Two Online Tests of Precognitive Remote Viewing* reported no significant precognition in its larger forced-choice experiment, but positive results in a separately judged free-response experiment; target interestingness influenced scores. Interpretation remains disputed. https://journals.lub.lu.se/jaex/article/view/24743
- 2025 Bancel et al., *A Forced-choice Precognition Experiment with Selected Cohorts*, two preregistered cohorts found no direct evidence of psi; an exploratory/tryout-variance result needs separate evaluation. https://journals.lub.lu.se/jaex/article/view/26394
- 2025 PLOS One transparent psi preregistered replication series, 26,483 participants and 420,472 trials in a **related but distinct precognition paradigm**, yielded a nominal effect not consistently replicated across the three studies; emphasizes control of confirmation and measurement bias. Not direct evidence about RV, but relevant method. https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0335330

## Initial preregistration proposal (requires external publication before collecting confirmatory data)
Research question: does a separately isolated observer exceed a 25% 4-way forced-choice match rate when blind independent judges compare fixed free-form descriptions with one randomly selected target and three same-domain decoys?

- Primary outcome: independent external judge selects the cryptographically specified target as best matching a sealed description among 4 randomly ordered candidates. Exactly ONE selection per trial. Require verifiable observation lock BEFORE candidate disclosure.
- H0: P(hit) = 1/4 for independent, exchangeable candidate positions.
- Primary statistic: preregister one-sided exact binomial p-value; prespecify alpha, trial count, a single judge protocol and exclusions. This is necessary but insufficient because candidate descriptions and judges may not be statistically exchangeable.
- Mandatory protocol: blind custody, public target commitment before viewing, independent timestamp of session commitment, no target feedback until batch ends, independent audience/chat transcript archived unedited, no target reuse during confirmatory phase, judges trained on neutral criteria but unexposed to outcomes, preregistered method and analysis code.
- Target balancing: exclude content that contains contextual metadata or predictable identifiers. All four options must be generated by same recipe and complexity/length distribution. Pilot should test judge baseline with deliberately irrelevant descriptions and matched null transcripts before any psi claim.
- Leakage red team: corrupt targets, change hashes, replay observations, submit early reveal, swap candidate index, try different paraphrases, look for shared memory or tool state. Report every test, not only successes.
- AI judges are exploratory only until calibrated against human judgments and null controls. Models can use generic semantic matching, prior chat context, or category cues; treat this as possible confounding, not proof of anomalies.
- Negative controls: randomly permuted assignment after observation, shuffled unrelated descriptions, decoy-only sets, delayed target selection variants, leakage canaries, and a predeclared conventional matching baseline.
- One successful trial means little: under H0 success probability is 1/4. Rigorous evidence requires repeatability across independent viewers, custodians, model versions, target pools and judges.
- The 2026 implementation is a LOCAL ENGINEERING PROTOTYPE. Public GitHub alone does not supply independent custody, safe target vault, irreversible timestamps or remote TLS portal. Confirmatory results require those external components.

## Why continue researching?
Not because historical data prove paranormal functioning; they do not establish an accepted mechanism. There are intriguing disputed observations, several changed protocols, potential experimental artifacts and strong incentives for independent reproduction. Cheap verifiable commitments, isolated agents, externally witnessed transcripts, and preregistration now allow more auditable falsification attempts than much older free-response work.

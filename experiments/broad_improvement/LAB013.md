# BEAN Lab 013: learning to revise, retain and recover

Status: experimental. Extends Lab 012 without modifying BEAN Core main, Bridge main, credentials, ERP data or hardware.

## Research question

When a policy selected by earlier synthetic training loses usefulness, can the experimental BEAN Bridge controller **notice verified counterevidence**, propose a more effective previously learned policy, reverse the decision if conditions recover, and retain the full evidence chain after restart?

## Additional parameter dimensions

Lab 012 optimizes four weights: surprise, contradiction, evidence coverage, and origin echo risk. Lab 013 adds seven bounded learning parameters:

| Parameter | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| audits_per_world | Uniform independent checks, mitigating self-selected verification bias |
| evidence_window | Recency window for comparing explanations |
| minimum_audits | Evidence threshold before a revision |
| advantage_margin | Required estimated benefit before switching |
| confirm_windows | Persistence requirement to reject one-off noise |
| cooldown_worlds | Avoid oscillation after a switch |
| max_policy_changes | Bound revisions and inspection complexity |

Three preset learning parameter combinations are evaluated on **training-only** timelines. A catalogue of alternatives is learned from the separate Lab 012 training distribution. New seeds are reserved for final normal, shifted, and recovered evaluations.

## Evidence and change control

The learner sees only observation IDs and feature values. It receives independently sampled verification labels, never the evaluator's full truth, stage labels, or hidden reward. All alternative policies are scored against the **same sampled evidence** using a paired inverse-propensity estimator. Selection is not a production authorization.

The policy-change record preserves (1) previous policy; (2) selected alternative; (3) estimated gain, rather than an invented certainty; (4) sampled evidence digests; (5) a falsifiable cause hypothesis; (6) a rollback target. Previously known strategies remain available. Exportable learning state retains every sampled observation and transition; checksums detect malformed snapshots.

## Acceptance gates

The predeclared success gate requires positive **net gain after audit costs**, no phase/domain regression beyond 0.75 reward units per world against the fixed trained policy, and policy revisions in at least three of four domains. This is a stringent synthetic benchmark, not evidence of AGI. Failure is retained as an outcome.

## Limits

- All worlds are simulated and binary-valued under four engineered features.
- Audit labels come from the synthetic evaluator. No real independently verified sensor or ERP observations are involved.
- A source-code-writing agent is not part of this experiment.
- The controller can change among a bounded list of already learned policies, not invent novel algorithms.
- Confidence intervals, richer hypotheses, sensor correlations, low-frequency change detection and real deployment remain future studies.
- Checksums show recorded-state consistency, not cryptographic authenticity of a maliciously replaced file.
- A legitimate self-improvement claim requires independent domains, multiple runs and genuine out-of-sample validation.
